ABOUT CORTEX CPM

Experience matters.
So does doing the work.

Cortex CPM is a small Australian business problem-solving and delivery practice built on more than three decades of experience in software engineering, project and program delivery, and complex business systems.

We combine that experience with hands-on work in modern technologies, including AI and automation—without assuming technology is always the answer.

Our approach is practical: understand the business need, work within real constraints, and deliver outcomes that justify the investment.

Human judgement, trust and responsibility remain essential—even when some of the work is performed by AI.
